🥋 STREET FIGHTER II - FIGHTER'S GUIDE & ROSTER

🎮 Universal Fighting Mechanics

  • Special Move Mode Toggles: Switch between 🟢 EASY (1-Key Shortcut) and 🟡 CLASSIC (Arcade Motion) independently for P1 (F1) and P2 (F2).
  • High / Mid Guard: Hold Back away from the opponent while they strike to block attacks.
  • Low Guard (Crouch Block): Hold Down + Back to block sweeps and low kicks.
  • Crouching Sweep: Press Crouch + Heavy Kick to execute a tripping sweep that knocks the opponent down.
  • ✈️ Flight Lift: Hold Up + tap Light Punch or Light Kick during jumps to gain mid-air upward thrust!
